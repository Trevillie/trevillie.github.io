About Me

I am a security researcher with Palo Alto Networks. I received my Ph.D. from UC Santa Barbara, where I worked in SecLab with Giovanni Vigna and Christopher Kruegel. Previously, I studied at ShanghaiTech University with Hao Chen and at Tsinghua University Department of Automation.

I'm passionate about cybersecurity in general. While my current focus is malware analysis, my past research involves smart contract analysis, DeFi anomaly detection, symbolic execution, and adversarial ML. I'm a proud member of the Shellphish hacking team.

I go by Hector with non-Chinese speakers. The photo is from 2018 (imagine me with less hair now).
